01 / Discover

Learn the Animal Words

Adjectives are words that describe people, animals, places, or things.

🦒

Tall → Taller

A giraffe is taller than a goat.

For most short adjectives, add -er.
🐘

Big → Bigger

An elephant is bigger than a rabbit.

For some words, double the final consonant.
🐢

Fast → Faster

A cheetah is faster than a tortoise.

Use than to compare two things.

Quick practice

The giraffe is _____ than the goat.
